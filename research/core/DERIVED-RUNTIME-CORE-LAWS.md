# Derived Runtime Core Laws

These are cross-programme syntheses. They are derived laws, not new numbered Foundations.

## Scope Conservation

`ClaimScope <= ActualSupportScope`.

Evidence, authority, enforcement, transaction participant set, controlled state, provider contract, identity/retention semantics, observation freshness and model scope all constrain what Runtime may claim.

## Identity / Lineage Conservation

Request, Operation, Attempt, physical realization/process, Effect, resource, provider receipt, lifecycle generation and evidence identities remain distinct unless an owning contract proves a mapping.

## Specification / Realization Non-Identity

A specification or operational commitment is not identical to its realization. Semantic identity may survive multiple physical realization topologies and executor populations.

## Local / Global Non-Lifting

Local completion, commit, visibility or participation does not imply global completion, publication, consensus or world effect.

## Non-Upgrading Composition

Combining weaker mechanisms does not automatically produce a stronger guarantee. Every composed guarantee needs an explicit composition argument and support scope.

## Typed Order Grammar

Program, causal, happens-before, visibility, persistence, recovery, queue, processing, barrier, lease/fencing and freshness orders are distinct projections. No universal total Runtime order is justified.

## History / Reproduction Non-Identity

Replay, restore, resume, reconstruction, migration and deterministic reproduction can preserve scoped equivalence without becoming the same historical occurrence. Recovery constructs an admissible continuation; it does not erase history.

## Evidence / Truth Non-Lifting

Traces, receipts, certificates, observations and records support scoped claims. They do not manufacture arbitrary world truth, authority or semantic Goal success.

## Orthogonality

Resource/capacity, authority/control, lifecycle/retention, semantic support/discharge, identity/lineage and physical realization interact but do not collapse into one Runtime state.

## Fresh-Execution Non-Necessity

A current result need or obligation may be discharged by validated historical support, cache/substitution, shared realization or incremental delta when the bound contract permits it. Fresh execution is not universally required.

## Open-World Re-grounding / Truth Convergence

After ambiguity or independently evolving external reality, restore/reattach alone is insufficient. Consequential repetition requires reconciliation against authoritative evidence and re-grounding of material state, authority and effect commitments.
