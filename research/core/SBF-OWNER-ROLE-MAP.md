## Runtime-retained SBF projection

The surviving Runtime-native projection across this history is deliberately thin:

- concrete Binding Contract at Operation admission;
- deferred concrete resolution under the admitted contract;
- shared / many-to-one realization and semantic accounting where it affects Runtime commitments;
- reuse/discharge of current Runtime obligations;
- Runtime-side invalidation and revalidation of previously supported claims after dependency change;
- concrete support/evidence/certificate consumption;
- lifecycle and standing consequences of binding/support changes.

Everything else is bridge or historical validation unless a future owner-boundary falsifier explicitly reopens the decision.
