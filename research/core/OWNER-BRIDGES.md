# Runtime Owner Bridges

Bridge classification prevents Runtime research from becoming shadow authority over adjacent owners.

## Runtime <-> SCD

**SCD owns:** abstract computational-description semantics; semantic observation/projection; semantic comparison/equivalence/refinement; semantic composition/context/open-interface structure; semantic transport/transformation preservation; abstraction/approximation and related description-level responsibilities.

**Runtime owns:** how an admitted Operation binds concrete referents/providers/implementations; deferred concrete resolution under a bound policy; concrete realization cardinality and shared realization; reuse/discharge; Runtime-side invalidation/revalidation; support/evidence and lifecycle consequences needed to realize or continue an Operation.

SBF2 and SBF3 are the clearest transferred abstract responsibilities. Other SBF claims are classified individually in [`SBF-OWNER-ROLE-MAP.md`](SBF-OWNER-ROLE-MAP.md).

## Runtime <-> World / Reality

**World owns:** referent ontology, world causation, property/measurement truth, external causal validity and physical/social truth conditions.

**Runtime owns:** structured Effects only where its contract genuinely controls them, plus the evidence and reconciliation boundary around those effects.

Observation, telemetry, records or certificates do not become Reality merely because Runtime produced them.

## Runtime <-> Host

**Host owns:** purposive Task continuity, Task-level composition and whether another Operation should be attempted within Host's bounded coordination role.

**Runtime owns:** the admitted operational commitment, its Attempts/realization, mechanical/effect evidence under Runtime-owned contracts and the need for reconciliation after ambiguity.

Runtime does not infer semantic Goal success from process success.

## Runtime <-> Harness / Agent

**Harness / Agent owns:** perception, reasoning, working set, action/tool selection, model invocation and cognitive-loop structure.

**Runtime owns:** the bounded operational commitment submitted for realization and its Runtime-side evidence/effect boundary.

Agent or model nondeterminism is not generic Runtime authority.

## Runtime <-> Network

**Network owns:** network/inter-locus capability, communicability, topology, forwarding/reachability and transport/network realization semantics.

**Runtime owns:** execution-side consumption of communication/coordination contracts and Runtime lifecycle consequences.

Network/Host may establish current-generation or split-brain coordination facts; LRF determines whether stale Runtime claims retain standing after an enforced frontier.
