# Runtime Foundation Families

These families are explanatory compression over preserved RF / EF / SBF / LRF identities. They are not new Foundation IDs and do not renumber provenance.

## 1. Operational Commitment & Realization

**Responsibility:** distinguish proposal/request, admitted Operation, Attempt, realization, execution occurrence and effect; preserve identity and lineage while allowing multiple or shared realization topologies.

**Primary provenance:** RF2–RF4; EF0, EF3, EF6, EF7, EF24, EF25; concrete Runtime projections of SBF0, SBF1, SBF4 and SBF6.

## 2. Boundary / Authority / Resource / Isolation

**Responsibility:** determine the boundary under which an Operation is meaningful and admissible; separate environment/context/dependency, capability/permission/control, capacity/admission/scheduling/backpressure and containment/isolation.

**Primary provenance:** RF6–RF9, RF13; EF10, EF19, EF22; related execution and cross-substrate falsification rounds.

## 3. Behavior / Interaction / Composition

**Responsibility:** represent typed time/order/causality, concurrency/nondeterminism/progress, interaction/communication, distributed views and contract-governed composition without assuming one total order or one structural tree.

**Primary provenance:** RF5, RF9, RF12, RF14; EF4, EF5, EF8–EF12, EF17, EF18, EF25; concrete Runtime-side composition consumption of SBF3.

## 4. Failure / Effect / Persistence / Reconciliation

**Responsibility:** model open-world effects, typed failure, partial/unknown outcomes, retry/idempotency/exactly-once claims, atomicity/compensation, persistence/recovery and post-ambiguity re-grounding.

**Primary provenance:** RF3, RF10, RF12, RF15; EF13–EF16, EF20, EF23; cross-reality and post-foundation tournaments.

## 5. Evidence / Claim / Truth Scope

**Responsibility:** keep occurrence, observation, record, evidence, certificate, claim and knowledge separate; constrain every Runtime claim to the support scope Runtime can actually establish.

**Primary provenance:** RF11, RF15, RF16; EF21; concrete Runtime evidence/support projections of SBF7, SBF8 and SBF12. Reality/source truth remains bridge-owned where applicable.

## 6. Lifecycle / Retention / Standing

**Responsibility:** represent retirement, visibility/usability, retention obligations, reclaimability, claim derivation/delegation/closure, validity domains, fencing and generation frontiers.

**Primary provenance:** LRF0–LRF3, with support from EF6, EF10, EF19, EF20, EF22 and concrete binding/lifecycle projections.

Core separations include:

- `Retirement != Reclamation`
- `ClaimExistence != ClaimStanding`
- `Reclaimable != Reclaimed`
- upper-layer reuse does not imply parent-substrate release
- extensional descendant closure does not imply intensional validity-domain fencing
