# Ordivon Runtime Research & Operational Realization Programme

This programme turns the completed Runtime research corpus into a durable, consumption-oriented research project.

It does **not** reopen numbered Runtime Foundation research by default.

## Mission

Maintain and develop Ordivon's theory of bounded operational realization; convert that theory into engineering doctrine, falsification methods and cross-owner principles; and reopen Foundation-level research only when a concrete falsifier demonstrates that the current theory is insufficient.

## Standing principle

The programme is governed by a consumption loop rather than an additive-Foundation loop:

```text
Theory
  -> Engineering consumption
  -> Dogfood / case study
  -> Falsification
  -> theory repair only when required
```

The frozen RF / EF / SBF / LRF registries remain provenance and current canonical evidence. They are not a backlog.
