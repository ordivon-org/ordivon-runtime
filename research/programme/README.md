# Ordivon Runtime Research & Operational Realization Programme

This programme turns the completed Runtime research corpus into a durable, consumption-oriented research project.

It does **not** reopen numbered Runtime Foundation research by default.

## Mission

Maintain and develop Ordivon's theory of bounded operational realization; convert that theory into engineering doctrine, falsification methods and cross-owner principles; and reopen Foundation-level research only when a concrete falsifier demonstrates that the current theory is insufficient.

## Programme structure

1. [`CONSTITUTION.md`](CONSTITUTION.md) — authority, frozen/evolving boundaries and admission rules.
2. [`THEORY-CORE.md`](THEORY-CORE.md) — stable theory surface and canonical dependencies.
3. [`ENGINEERING-CONSUMPTION.md`](ENGINEERING-CONSUMPTION.md) — how theory becomes minimal engineering consequences.
4. [`FALSIFIER-LIBRARY.md`](FALSIFIER-LIBRARY.md) — reusable attacks against Runtime designs and claims.
5. [`DOGFOOD-AND-CASE-STUDIES.md`](DOGFOOD-AND-CASE-STUDIES.md) — concrete domains that test the theory.
6. [`EMERGING-RESEARCH.md`](EMERGING-RESEARCH.md) — Operational Realization Theory, Standing/Currentness Semantics and Operational Claim Theory candidates.
7. [`REOPEN-CONDITIONS.md`](REOPEN-CONDITIONS.md) — exact conditions for reopening frozen theory.
8. [`consumption/C1-STANDING-CURRENTNESS-RECOVERY.md`](consumption/C1-STANDING-CURRENTNESS-RECOVERY.md) — first real consumption/falsification wave: typed currentness under historical continuity, owner authority and source advancement.

## Standing principle

The programme is governed by a consumption loop rather than an additive-Foundation loop:

```text
Theory
  -> Engineering consumption
  -> Dogfood / case study
  -> Falsification
  -> theory repair only when required
```

The frozen RF / EF / SBF / LRF registries remain provenance and current canonical evidence. They are not a backlog.
