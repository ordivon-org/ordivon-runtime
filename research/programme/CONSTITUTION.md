# Runtime Research Programme Constitution v1

## 1. Owner

The programme belongs to the Ordivon Runtime semantic owner and studies bounded operational realization.

Runtime owns admitted operational commitment, Attempt/realization lineage, Runtime-controlled authority/resource/effect/evidence semantics, reconciliation and standing of Runtime-owned claims.

It does not absorb Host Goal/Task continuity, Harness cognition, SCD description semantics, Network inter-locus ontology, or World/Reality truth.

## 2. Frozen canonical layer

The following remain frozen absent an admitted reopen condition:

- RF0–RF16;
- EF0–EF26;
- SBF0–SBF23 identities/history with current role reclassification;
- LRF0–LRF3;
- six owner-native Foundation Families;
- current derived Runtime laws, subject to version-forward clarification or repair rather than silent mutation.

EF27 remains superseded historical evidence. SBF24 and LRF4 do not exist.

`WholeRuntimeExhaustiveClosure = UNKNOWN` remains the correct closure claim.

## 3. Evolving layer

The programme may continuously evolve:

- engineering-consumption analyses;
- falsifier scenarios and regression cases;
- dogfood/case-study evidence;
- cross-owner bridge clarifications that do not change another owner's authority;
- emerging-theory notes;
- explicit version-forward repairs supported by falsification evidence.

These are not new Foundations merely because they are new findings.

## 4. Required research-to-engineering boundary

Research does not directly change production Runtime behavior.

Every engineering consumption must state:

1. concrete consumer/problem;
2. relevant theory/laws;
3. exact failure or ambiguity being prevented;
4. minimum engineering consequence;
5. falsifier/test;
6. observed result;
7. whether theory was confirmed, narrowed, or falsified.

No wholesale rewrite follows from theoretical elegance alone.

## 5. Research admission rule

A new Runtime research route must identify at least one of:

- a concrete production or dogfood falsifier unexplained by the current theory;
- a contradiction between current Runtime laws;
- a newly observed class of realization that current ontology cannot represent without identity/scope/support/standing collapse;
- a cross-owner boundary conflict that creates dual or missing authority;
- evidence that a current law materially overclaims or underclaims its valid scope.

General novelty, analogy, or unused literature is insufficient for Foundation reopening.

## 6. Core methodological discipline

Prefer:

```text
consumer trace
  -> causal falsifier
  -> minimal semantic repair
  -> minimal engineering migration
  -> regression evidence
```

over theory-driven production rewrites.

Preserve historical identity and provenance. Repairs are version-forward; history is not cosmetically rewritten.

## 7. Cross-owner export rule

Runtime may export laws such as Scope Conservation, Identity/Lineage Conservation, Local/Global Non-Lifting and Standing/Currentness distinctions as reusable principles.

Consumption by another owner does not transfer that owner's semantic authority to Runtime.

## 8. Stop rule

When no admitted falsifier is active, Foundation-level Runtime research stays stopped. The programme continues through consumption, dogfood, regression and observation rather than additive ontology production.
